**Project:** Premium, simple‑yet‑elegant real estate homepage for Marci Metzger, a Pahrump, Nevada realtor (The Ridge Realty Group).

**Audience:** Home buyers and sellers in Southern Nevada.

**Feel & Tone:** Warm, trustworthy, upscale but approachable, desert‑inspired.

**Palette:** Warm neutrals – sand, cream, deep charcoal – with **ONE** accent color (to be chosen).

**Typography:** Elegant serif for headings, clean sans‑serif for body copy.

**Restrictions:** No purple/cyan gradients, no identical card grids, avoid generic AI copy, no emoji icons.

**Goals:**
- Hero section with striking desert backdrop and headline.
- Clear call‑to‑action for property search and contact.
- Featured listings displayed in a staggered, non‑grid layout.
- Quick‑info sections (testimonials, agent intro) with visual hierarchy.
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

**Deliverables:**
- `index.html` and accompanying CSS/SCSS.
- Asset placeholders for hero image and accent‑color palette.
- Optional `PRODUCT.md` (this file) and a future `DESIGN.md` documenting design decisions.
