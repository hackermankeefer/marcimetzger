# Design reference notes — Marci Metzger homepage revamp

## Design goal

Improve the existing Marci Metzger / The Ridge Realty Group homepage. Preserve its actual Pahrump Realtor identity, logo, photos, copy, contact details, and familiar homepage content. Use visual references to improve presentation and usability; do not turn the page into a new luxury real-estate developer brand.

## Existing site structure and content

- Header with the existing identity, navigation, and call action.
- “Pahrump Realtor” hero.
- Marci Metzger profile and nearly three decades of experience.
- Seller results and selling guidance.
- Buyer guidance.
- Home search and listings.
- Photo gallery.
- Services.
- Contact form, address, phone, hours, map, social links, and footer.

The local snapshot is a generated GoDaddy export. Some image assets and form/search behavior remain hosted by GoDaddy. The new static prototype links back to the live listings and contact flows rather than pretending to implement their backends.

## What the supplied visual references contribute

- **Modern Real Estate Website UI Design:** full-width property photography, clear navigation, a small trust strip, and a well-organized featured-property rhythm.
- **LeonHome:** confident editorial type, dramatic scale, asymmetrical imagery, and clear sections.
- **SteelForm:** image-forward composition, restrained interface details, and a strong photo story.
- **The Ridge Realty Group ZIPs:** the Pahrump brokerage site reinforces the deep navy and white identity, Lato and Lusitana typography, clear broker credentials, team context, and direct office/contact details. The other ZIP is a separate Orange County business; its useful cues are its direct buyer/seller entry points and testimonial/social proof, not its location, phone number, agent roster, or property imagery.

Borrow the principles—image hierarchy, confident typography, spacing, and section rhythm—not the example brands, claims, property inventory, or exact layouts.

Treat the supplied ZIPs as visual/content references only. Use Marci's confirmed brokerage identity and Pahrump information. Do not transfer the other businesses' logos, staff/team photos, testimonial photos, Orange County property images, phone numbers, or claims into this site. Service-card image crops currently come from the user's own reference screenshot as specifically requested.

## Design decisions for the revamp

- Use the Ridge Realty Group's Lato body type and Lusitana display type to bring the personal homepage into the brokerage's visual family.
- Use the Pahrump brokerage's deep navy and white identity, with restrained blue accents and Marci's existing content and photography.
- Make Marci's Nevada broker license and official Ridge Realty Group relationship easy to see and link to the Pahrump brokerage page.
- Use the supplied Pahrump site’s round Ridge Realty Group mark in the trust strip, and include the office line as a secondary contact beside Marci’s direct number.
- Retain the original logo, source-site imagery, Marci portrait, Pahrump references, and official contact details.
- Use a dark blue photographic hero inspired by the existing homepage, with cleaner navigation and clearer calls to action.
- Organize the current buyer and seller guidance into distinct, easy-to-scan paths.
- Retain the listings, gallery, services, history, and contact sections; remove repetitive presentation.
- Identify 2021 sales/client results as historical.
- Link search and contact actions to the original live pages because their hosted GoDaddy/MLS behavior is not part of the static snapshot.
- Keep mobile navigation, gallery controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts.

## Copy and claims

Base biography and service copy on the official site. Do not invent property listings, testimonials, awards, service areas, or performance statistics. The current source says Marci has helped buyers and sellers in many markets since 1995 and reports nearly 90 clients / $28.5 million in 2021 sales; keep the sales figures dated if they are used.
